Brooke's Top Five Musicals

This is my personal ranking, don't get upset.

1. Les Miserables

I love this show. I saw it in person.

Watch on YouTube

2. Hairspray

An amazing movie/musical and the cast includes Zac Efron, Brittany Snow, and John Travolta, to name a few.

Watch on YouTube

3. High School Musical

My childhood STAPLE movie. Something that both my brother and sister approved, so it was played a lot in our household. And who didn't have a crush on Zac Efron when they were a little girl?

Watch on YouTube

4. The Phantom of the Opera

When I was younger, this movie really scared me. But over the years I have come to see the beauty of it and the grace in which the music plays such a story and creates another world between the two main characters.

Watch on YouTube

5. Mamma Mia

Saved the best for last. Will forever be my favorite musical ever. Such a romantic, summer, feel-good movie with a stacked cast and beautiful voices.

Watch on YouTube
