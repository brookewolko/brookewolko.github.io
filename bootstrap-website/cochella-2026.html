Cochella 2026

Here is the link to the Instagram post that announced last year's Cochella musical lineup.

  • The headliners included Sabrina Carptener, Justin Bieber, and Karol G.
  • This cultivated a lot of excitement around Justin Bieber performing because he has not performed or gone on tour in many years, so for a lot of fans who grew up with him, this could be their only chance to see him live. He played many of his old songs, which fans loved and for his hit song One Less Lonely Girl, the 'lonely girl' was none other than the famous singer Billie Eilish.
  • Sabrina Carptenter brought out Madonna for her closing act during her weekend two performance, and the two sang Like A Prayer by Madonna.
  • Another cool thing, the festival has multiple stages across the dessert area, so you can see so many different artists throughout the entire festival, and bounce around if there is one you want to see more than the other.

Fun Fact: after the lineup was announced, tickets sold out in less than one week!
