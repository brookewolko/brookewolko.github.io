About

This website is meant to help educate those who are unaware what Coachella is and entails. It occurred just last month in April 2026 and has been talk of the town since.

  • Coachella is one of the biggest and most attended music festivals in the world.
  • Officially, it is the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.
  • It takes place every year in Indio, California in the dessert.
  • It occurs for two weekends in a row with a week of rest/break/partying for attendees in between. In 2027, it is April 9th to the 11th and again the next weekend April 16th to the 18th.
  • Fun Fact: many people camp in their cars or in tents all throughout the parking area for the weekend or two weeks they are there.

It often features a wide range of musical artists from many different genres and levels of stardom, as well as large-scale art projects and some interactive ones too.

Personally, I unfortunately have never attended Coachella, but I am an avid online viewer and stalker of all Coachella content. For even more information, visit the official Coachella website.
