Do you enjoy being outside? Perhaps, you LOVE to listen to music?

Coachella is the place for you and your friends. This two weekend long music festival is calling your name. On this website you will find information about the what Coachella entails, fun facts about it, the musical guest lineup of last year's festival, and more. Be sure to explore to find out all there is to know about Coachella.

Due to the fact that Coachella just happened, publicity for it has been very popular the past month and more people are learning about a) what the festival is and b) what an amazing experience it can be.

Famous influencers are also known to post about their experience which is a big way that outsiders are able to see what occurs during and on the festival grounds. These include names like Alix Earle, Julia Mervis, Jake Shane, and more, along with celebrities like Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner, just to name a few.
